Discrete Op Amp Design

LTSpice Execution KiCad PCB Design Analog Circuit Design

Project Overview

This engineering project was undertaken to translate core semiconductor theory into physical execution. The circuit architecture is based on principles explored within Analysis and Design of Analog Integrated Circuits by Gray, Hurst, Lewis, and Meyer. It explores the foundational internal topologies of operational amplifiers, specifically analyzing differential input stages, current mirror loads, voltage amplification stages, and class-AB output buffers.

Initial evaluations on a solderless breadboard exposed expected parasitic capacitance problems and high-frequency noise interference inherent to transient prototyping environments. To eliminate these layout-induced variables and achieve clean validation results, a custom two-layer integrated PCB was designed in KiCad using strict engineering layout constraints to optimize trace routing and ground-plane partitioning.
